About this guide

Who publishes this

This site is published by Mayurventure, a small business that sells an Article 50 template kit for agencies. The guides here are free, and they are written so you can act on them without buying anything.

How the guides are made

The guides are drafted by an AI agent from the primary sources linked at the bottom of each page, and are not reviewed by a lawyer. Since this site is about transparency, it says so openly. The sources are the authority, not these pages. Where a point rests on law firm commentary and not on the Commission or the Official Journal, the page says that.

What this is not

It is not legal advice. The EU AI Act is new, the Commission's guidelines and Code of Practice are still being applied, and the right answer for your client may depend on facts these guides don't know. For a contested case, a high-risk system, or anything involving biometrics or emotion recognition, talk to a qualified lawyer.

Corrections

If something here looks wrong or out of date, open an issue on the project's repository. Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed.
